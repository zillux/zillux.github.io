ClipStack privacy policy

ClipStack is a desktop app that runs on the user's own computer. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share anyone's information.

YouTube

ClipStack uses YouTube API Services. When you connect a YouTube channel, Google gives ClipStack a permission token. ClipStack uses it only to look up the connected channel's name and to upload and schedule the videos you choose on that channel. The token is stored only on the computer where you connected the channel, and it is never sent anywhere except to Google.

You can remove ClipStack's access at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps & services, or by pressing Disconnect in ClipStack.

See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
